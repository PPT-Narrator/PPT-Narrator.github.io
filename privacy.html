Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Overview

Tingbo: PPT Notes Reader (“the App”, “we”, “us”) is an iOS app that reads the speaker notes in your presentations aloud, slide by slide, to help you rehearse before you present.

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is handled. Presentations and notes are stored on your device, except when you use a conversion service as described below. Firebase-enabled iOS versions use Firebase Analytics for feature usage; we do not include file names or note text in our custom analytics events.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require an account. During normal use, we do not ask you to provide:

  • Your name, email address, or contact details, unless you choose to contact us.
  • Access to your contacts or photos.
  • Presentation content, file names, or speaker-note text as App analytics events.

Firebase Analytics processes usage events and related technical information in Firebase-enabled iOS versions, as described below. A presentation may be sent to a conversion service when you use cloud conversion.

Local Processing by Default

Your presentation files and speaker notes are processed on your device. Imported presentations, slide images, and notes are stored locally in the App’s storage so you can reopen them anytime. Reading the notes aloud uses the built-in iOS text-to-speech on your device.

Presentation Conversion

To render every page of a PPT or PPTX file as a slide image, the App may perform a conversion step:

  • Local conversion (default): The App connects to a conversion service running on your own local network (for example, a service on your Mac). Your file is sent over your local network and is not transmitted to any third party.
  • Cloud conversion: When a presentation needs remote rendering, the current iOS build requests a conversion job through a Cloudflare Worker, then uploads the selected file directly to CloudConvert. The file is used only to complete the conversion you requested and is not used for any other purpose.

Your file is never used for advertising, profiling, or anything other than the conversion you explicitly trigger.

Third-Party Services

When cloud conversion is used, Cloudflare handles the job request and CloudConvert receives the selected presentation to create the rendered result. These services receive only the information and file needed for that operation. We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process standard server logs (such as your IP address) in accordance with GitHub’s privacy practices.

App and Website Analytics

Firebase-enabled iOS versions use Firebase Analytics to log file format and import source, rendering results and duration, slide count, slide navigation, and narration-start events. Our custom events do not include file names, presentations, or speaker-note text. Firebase may also collect automatic app events and device or app-instance identifiers. See Firebase’s event documentation and privacy information.

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure page views, visits, referral sources, and page performance. The service does not use analytics cookies or track individual visitors across websites. See Cloudflare’s explanation of Web Analytics.

Website analytics is separate from the App’s Firebase Analytics and cannot access presentations, speaker notes, or audio stored in the App.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your imported presentations remain stored locally in the App. You can delete any presentation from your history at any time. To remove all data, delete the App from your device, which removes its locally stored content.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
