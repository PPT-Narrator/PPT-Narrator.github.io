Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Overview

PPT Narrator: Speaker Notes (“the App”, “we”, “us”) is an iOS app that reads the speaker notes in your presentations aloud, slide by slide, to help you rehearse before you present.

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is handled. In short: the App is designed to keep your presentations and notes on your own device, and we do not collect, sell, or share any personal information.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require you to create an account. We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact information.
  • Your location, contacts, or photos.
  • Any analytics, usage tracking, or advertising identifiers.
  • Any content of your presentations or speaker notes.

Local Processing by Default

Your presentation files and speaker notes are processed on your device. Imported presentations, slide images, and notes are stored locally in the App’s storage so you can reopen them anytime. Reading the notes aloud uses the built-in iOS text-to-speech on your device.

Presentation Conversion

To render every page of a PPT or PPTX file as a slide image, the App may perform a conversion step:

  • Local conversion (default): The App connects to a conversion service running on your own local network (for example, a service on your Mac). Your file is sent over your local network and is not transmitted to any third party.
  • Cloud conversion (optional): If the developer has configured a cloud conversion endpoint, your presentation file may be transmitted to that service (a third-party file-conversion provider) for the sole purpose of rendering your slides. The file is used only to complete the conversion you requested and is not used for any other purpose.

Your file is never used for advertising, profiling, or anything other than the conversion you explicitly trigger.

Third-Party Services

When the optional cloud conversion is used, a third-party file-conversion provider may process the presentation file solely to convert it into slide images. This provider receives only the file you choose to convert and nothing else. We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process standard server logs (such as your IP address) in accordance with GitHub’s privacy practices.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your imported presentations remain stored locally in the App. You can delete any presentation from your history at any time. To remove all data, delete the App from your device, which removes its locally stored content.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
